About Us

⚔️

Late-Game Focused

Our members are mostly experienced, late-game players who know their way around Gielinor. Whether you're grinding bosses or going for max, you'll fit right in.

🎉

Weekly Events

We run weekly competitions and events through Wise Old Man — skill of the week, boss of the week, and more. There's always something going on.

💬

Social First

We're a social clan first and foremost. Good vibes, active Discord, people actually talking in CC. No elitism, no toxicity, just a chill place to play.

🛡️

No Drama

We keep it simple — respect each other, follow the rules, and have fun. Staff handles issues quickly so you can focus on what matters: the game.

Clan Rules

Keep it simple. Keep it respectful.

1

Follow All Official Rules

All Discord Terms of Service and Old School RuneScape rules apply. Breaking them will result in action.

2

No Politics or Religion

This server is for OSRS, not debate clubs. Keep political and religious discussions out of all channels.

3

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with basic respect. Harassment, hate speech, racism, and sexism will not be tolerated.

4

No Trolling or Ragebaiting

Excessive trolling, instigating arguments, or baiting others for a reaction will lead to warnings or bans.

5

No Scamming or Luring

Any attempts to scam, lure, or manipulate other players will result in an instant ban — no warnings.

6

No Begging

Don't ask for free items, GP, or services. This includes both direct messages and public channels.

7

Respect the Staff

Don't argue with moderators or admins in public. If you have a concern, send a private message.

8

No Sexual Harassment

Inappropriate comments, advances, or behavior towards other users will not be tolerated.
